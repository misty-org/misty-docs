# Plans and Boosts

Misty Cloud is free to use. Misty+ gives your account more storage. Boosts raise a space's level for everyone in it.

> Plans and Boosts only apply on Misty Cloud. A server you run yourself has no plans or levels, and every space on it is unlimited.

## Compare plans

- **Free** has 5 GB of storage. You can make and join as many spaces as you like.
- **Misty+** costs $8 a month or $72 a year. It has 100 GB of storage, files up to 100 MB, and 3 Boosts to give.
- A **Boost** costs $2 a month or $18 a year and raises one space's level.
- Prices are in US dollars, plus tax where it applies. See [Pricing](https://mistysys.com/pricing) for the full comparison.

## Get Misty+

1. Go to [Pricing](https://mistysys.com/pricing) and sign in.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Select **Get Misty+** and pay with Stripe.

In the app, you can also open **Plan and billing** from your account menu.

## Boost a space

Each Boost counts toward the space's level. Boosts from everyone in the space add up.

1. In the app, open the space's **⋯** menu, choose **People** and select **Boost**. Or go to **Boosts** in your account settings on the Misty website and select **Boost** next to the space.
2. Set how many Boosts to give. With Misty+, use your included Boosts first.
3. Select **Save**, or **Continue to payment** when you're buying Boosts for the first time.

- A Space reaches Level 1 at 2 Boosts, Level 2 at 7 and Level 3 at 14. See [Space levels](#/reference/space-levels) for what each level allows.
- Each Boost past a level adds 5 GB of shared storage.
- You can move your included Misty+ Boosts to another space once a week.
- Only Boosts from current members count. If you leave a space, your Boosts stop counting there.

## Change or cancel

1. Open **Subscription** in your account settings on the Misty website.
2. Select **Switch to yearly** or **Switch to monthly** to change how you're billed. The change takes effect right away, and the difference is charged or credited.
3. Select **Manage** to update your card or download invoices.
4. Select **Cancel** to stop Misty+ at the end of the period you've paid for.

To change bought Boosts, open **Boosts**, select the space and change the number. Setting it to 0 ends those Boosts at the end of the month.

## When a plan or Boost ends

- Your account moves to Free, with 5 GB of storage.
- A Space drops to the level its remaining Boosts reach.
- Nothing is deleted. If you're over a limit, you can still open, download and delete everything. You can't add more until you're under the limit.
