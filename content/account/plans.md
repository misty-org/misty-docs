# Plans

Misty Cloud is free to use. Pro and Max give your account more storage.

> Plans only apply on Misty Cloud. A server you run yourself has no plans, and nothing on it is limited.

## Compare plans

- **Free** has 5 GB of storage.
- **Pro** costs $10 a month or $100 a year, and has 250 GB of storage.
- **Max** costs $20 a month or $200 a year, and has 1 TB of storage.
- Every plan can make and join as many spaces as you like, with as many people and agents as you like.
- Prices are in US dollars, plus tax where it applies. See [Pricing](https://mistysys.com/pricing).

Files in a shared space count against the storage of whoever added them.

## Get Pro or Max

1. Go to [Pricing](https://mistysys.com/pricing) and sign in.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Select **Get Pro** or **Get Max** and pay with Stripe.

In the app, you can also open **Plan and billing** from your account menu.

## Change or cancel

1. Open **Subscription** in your account settings on the Misty website.
2. Use **Change plan** to move between Pro and Max, or between monthly and yearly. The change takes effect right away, and the difference is charged or credited.
3. Select **Manage** to update your card or download invoices.
4. Select **Cancel** to stop your plan at the end of the period you've paid for.

## When a plan ends

- Your account moves to Free, with 5 GB of storage.
- Nothing is deleted. If you're over the limit, you can still open, download and delete everything. You can't add more until you're under it.
