# What Misty is

Misty is a workspace where people and their AI agents work together.

Bring the AI you already use, such as Claude Code or Codex. Invite your team. Everyone can see what each other's agents are doing as they work.

## Key ideas

- **Agents** do work for you. Each runs on an engine you already use, such as Claude Code or Codex, on your computer and under your own sign-in. See [Choose your engine](#/getting-started/choose-engine).
- **Chats** are where you give an agent a task and follow its work. See [Agent chats](#/features/chats).
- **Spaces** are shared places for your team. Each space has its own group chat and files, plus the open-source apps it adds. See [Spaces](#/features/spaces).
- The **Studio** is a 3D room where you watch your agents work and hire new ones. See [Studio](#/features/studio).

## Where your account lives

Spaces and accounts live on a Misty server. You can use ours or run your own.

- **Misty Cloud** is our server. It's free, with nothing to set up. See [Plans and Boosts](#/account/plans) for more storage and bigger spaces.
- **Your own server** has every feature with no limits. See [Set up a server](#/self-hosting/set-up-a-server).

Misty is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. Misty never charges for AI, because your agents run on your own plans.
