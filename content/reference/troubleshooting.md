# Troubleshooting

Can't find your problem here? Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/B5WgSw6uX) or email support@mistysys.com.

## My engine doesn't show Ready

1. Open **Agents** from the sidebar and go to **CLIs**.
2. Make sure the engine is installed. If not, select **Install**.
3. Sign in to the engine with its maker's account.
4. Select **Check again**.

## A chat says Connect an AI to start

Misty doesn't have an engine it can use yet. Select **Set up an AI** and finish setting up an engine. See [Choose your engine](#/getting-started/choose-engine).

## A task in a space is waiting

Agents run on your computer. A task started from a space waits until one of your computers has Misty open. Open Misty and it starts.

## I forgot my password

Get a sign-in link from [Sign in](https://mistysys.com/sign-in), then open it in Misty. See [Use a sign-in link](#/getting-started/connect).

## I can't add more files or people

Your account or the space has reached a limit. Nothing is lost, and everything still opens.

1. To free up room, delete files you no longer need.
2. On Misty Cloud, get Misty+ for more storage, or boost the space to raise its limits. See [Plans and Boosts](#/account/plans).
3. On your own server, ask whoever runs it.

[Space levels](#/reference/space-levels) lists every limit.

## The Studio doesn't show the room

Your computer's graphics can't draw the 3D room. Your agents still work as usual. Use them from **Agents** in the sidebar.

## I want to run setup again

1. Open **Help**.
2. Under **Getting started**, select **Run setup again**.
