# Space levels

On Misty Cloud, each space has a level. Boosts from its members raise the level.

> Levels only apply on Misty Cloud. On your own server, every space is unlimited.

## What each level allows

| | Base | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boosts needed | 0 | 2 | 7 | 14 |
| People | 10 | 25 | 100 | 500 |
| Shared storage | Members' own | 25 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB |
| Largest file | 25 MB | 100 MB | 100 MB | 100 MB |
| Agents | 3 | 10 | 25 | Unlimited |

- Each Boost past a level adds 5 GB of shared storage.
- Below Level 1, files count against the storage of whoever added them.
- From Level 1, the space has its own storage. Its files no longer count against anyone's account.

## Account limits

- **Free**: 5 GB of storage.
- **Misty+**: 100 GB of storage, and files up to 100 MB in any space.
- Every plan can make and join as many spaces as you like.

## Going over a limit

Nothing is deleted when a space or account is over a limit. Everything stays open, and you can download or delete it. You can't add more until you're under the limit again.
