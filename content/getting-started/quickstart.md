# Quickstart

This guide takes you from installing Misty to working with your first agent and teammate. It takes about ten minutes.

## Before you start

You'll need an engine you can sign in to. Claude Code works with a Claude plan, and Codex works with a ChatGPT account. Antigravity is free with a Google account, and OpenCode needs no account at all.

## 1. Install Misty

1. Download Misty for your computer from [Download](https://mistysys.com/download).
2. Open the app. You'll see **Welcome to Misty**.

## 2. Create your account

1. Select **Get started**.
2. Select **Continue with Misty Cloud**. To use a server you run yourself, select **Use my own server** instead. See [Connect to a server](#/getting-started/connect).
3. Enter your email and a password, or continue with Google.

## 3. Set up your engine

After you sign in, Misty walks you through setup.

1. Read **How Misty works** and select **Continue**.
2. Under **Pick your default engine**, choose one. Select **Install** or **Sign in** next to it until it shows **Ready**.
3. Select **Continue**.
4. Under **Which apps should agents use?**, sign in to any apps you want your agents to use, such as Gmail or Slack. You can skip this for now.
5. Select **Start using Misty**.

[Choose your engine](#/getting-started/choose-engine) explains each engine and what it costs.

## 4. Give your agent a task

1. Select **New** in the sidebar.
2. Type what you want done in the message box, such as "Summarize the files in my Documents folder".
3. Press Enter. Your agent starts working, and you can follow each step.

[Agent chats](#/features/chats) covers attaching files, approving plans and stopping a run.

## 5. Invite your team

Spaces are where you work with other people. Your account comes with one.

1. Open **Space** from the sidebar.
2. Open the **⋯** menu and choose **People**.
3. Select **Invite member**, enter your teammate's email and select **Invite**.
4. Select **Copy** and send them the link. Once they join, you can chat, share files and see each other's agents at work.

> Agents run on your computer, so Misty needs to be open for them to work. A task someone starts for you in a space waits until one of your computers is online.
