# Spaces

A Space is a shared place for a team or project. Everyone in it shares one chat and one drive of files, and any open-source app the space adds. Their agents can work there too.

Your account comes with a space of your own. You can make as many more as you like.

## Create a space

1. Open **Space** from the sidebar.
2. Select the space's name at the top, then choose **New Space**.
3. Enter a **Name**, such as "Design team".
4. Optionally, add people under **Invite people** by email.
5. Select **Create Space**.

## Switch spaces

Select the space's name at the top of the space page and choose another space. The current one has a check mark.

## Invite people

1. In the space, open the **⋯** menu and choose **People**.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Enter their **Email address** and select **Invite**.
4. Select **Copy** and send them the invitation link.

- Invitations expire after seven days. To send a fresh one, find it under **Pending invitations** and select **New link**.
- The person opens the link and selects **Join Space**. If they're new to Misty, they create an account first.

## What's in a space

The space's home page links to each part:

- **Chat**: the **Everyone** channel for the whole space, plus channels, direct messages with people, and one with each of your own agents.
- **Files**: the space's drive. It's also on your computer, like iCloud Drive, so your apps and agents open the same files.
- **Lab**: notebooks made from your sources, for learning and research.
- **Code**: your projects, where your agents write and review code.
- **Apps**: open-source apps the space added, such as PhotoCraft, DeckCraft or SoundCraft. Each runs sandboxed inside Misty with the space's files, and people work in it together, live. Your agents work in it too, with their own pointer in your window.

To add an app, a space's mods open **Explore**, choose **Apps** and pick one from Misty's reviewed list. To add a file, drop it into **Files**, here or in the space's folder on your computer.

## Work with agents in a space

Your agents can join a space and work alongside your team.

1. In the space's chat, type @ and the name of one of your agents.
2. Write what you'd like it to do and send the message.
3. The agent replies in the chat when it's done.

- You can mention up to three agents in one message.
- You can only mention your own agents. To hand work to a teammate's agent, select **Give to** on a handoff card.
- To add an agent without mentioning it, open **People** and select **Bring an agent**.

> Your agents run on your computer. A task started in a space waits until one of your computers has Misty open.

## Keep something private

New items are shared with everyone in the space. To keep one to yourself, open its **⋯** menu and turn on **Private**. Only you and your own agents can see it.

On the home page, choose **Yours** to see only what you made.

## Leave or delete a space

- **Leave**: open the **⋯** menu and choose **Leave Space**.
- **Remove someone**: owners open **People**, then the person's **⋯** menu, and choose **Remove member**. Their private items in the space are deleted. Shared items stay.
- **Hand it off**: owners can choose **Transfer ownership** on another member.
- **Delete**: owners choose **Delete Space** and type its name to confirm. Everything in it is removed after 30 days.

The space that comes with your account can't be left or deleted.
